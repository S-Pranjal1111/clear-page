Questions

How do I receive my guide?

Checkout is handled securely by Creem. After purchase you download your PDF, then print it whenever you like.

Can I print more than one copy?

Yes. You may print as many copies as you need for your own personal use. The file may not be resold, shared or redistributed. See the Terms of Service for details.

What if it isn’t right for me?

Our refund policy explains how refunds work. If you have a question first, write to support@clearpage.online.

Can I ask for a guide on a specific topic?

Yes. Email us at support@clearpage.online with the problem you need solved. We use your suggestions to decide what to make next.
