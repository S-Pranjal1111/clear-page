Paper works when everything else doesn’t.

  • Hand it to a paramedic

    The Emergency Sheet puts conditions, allergies, medications, contacts and your wishes on one page. Keep a copy on the fridge and one in your bag.

  • Nothing to log in to

    No app, no subscription, no battery. Print it once, then siblings, aides and neighbors can find what they need in seconds.

  • Private by design

    The binder asks only for the last four digits of account numbers, and for where the full details are kept. A misplaced folder never exposes a password or a Social Security number.

A little structure now saves hours later.

    Use a home printer or a copy shop. Hole-punch the pages and file them in a 1.5-inch binder with tab dividers.

  2. 2

    Fill in the Emergency Sheet

    Start with page 3. Make two copies: one for the fridge, one for your bag or car.

    These are the pages you will open most. Write in pencil and update after every appointment.

    Use the roles and weekly coverage pages to split the work fairly, before anyone feels stretched.

Who it’s for

Anyone who is keeping track of another person’s care, usually on top of everything else.

  • Adult children coordinating care

    You are juggling work, your own family and a parent’s appointments. The binder keeps what you learn in one place, so you are not starting from scratch at every phone call.

  • A spouse or partner

    You know the routines by heart, but someone else may need to step in one day. The binder writes it all down so that they can.

  • Siblings sharing the load

    Family roles, weekly coverage and a meeting planner help you divide the work fairly and keep everyone on the same page.

Questions before you buy

Which paper size should I choose?

Choose the size your printer uses. A4 (210 × 297 mm) is standard across Europe, Asia, Africa and Oceania. US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) is standard in the United States and Canada. The content is identical.

What exactly do I receive?

One PDF of 31 pages in the size you choose. Print it at home or at a copy shop, and print extra copies of any page whenever you need them.

Can I type into the PDF?

It is designed to be filled in by hand, ideally in pencil so entries are easy to update. The pages are not fillable forms, but you can print a fresh copy of any page when one fills up.

Does it work outside the United States?

Nearly every page does: medications, contacts, appointments, daily care, home and family pages work anywhere. The insurance pages, the legal checklist and the Helpful Resources page mention US programs and helplines such as Medicare and the Eldercare Locator, and leave space for your own local numbers.

What if I order the wrong paper size?

If you have not printed it yet, email support@clearpage.online within 7 days of purchase and we will swap it for the other edition at no charge. See the refund policy for the full details.

Is it useful if my parent has dementia?

It includes prompts for it: notes for emergency responders on memory and communication, a wandering and memory section in the home safety checklist, and a reminder to tell hospital staff about dementia and delirium risk. It is an organizer, not a care program.
